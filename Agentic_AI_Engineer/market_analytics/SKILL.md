# Market Analytics Skill

This skill analyzes California real estate market conditions.

Use this skill for requests such as:

- "What is the housing market like in Irvine?"
- "Is it a buyer's or seller's market in Newport Beach?"
- "How are prices trending in Anaheim?"
- "How much are homes selling for in Irvine?"
- "Are buyers negotiating in Mission Viejo?"
- "What is the average days on market in Tustin?"

The skill:

1. Retrieves historical sold market data.
2. Calculates average and median prices.
3. Analyzes price-per-square-foot trends.
4. Computes days-on-market trends.
5. Calculates list-to-close price ratios.
6. Compares active inventory with sold volume.
7. Generates a market summary with insights.
